Optional Premium subscription. Microphone is used only while measuring. Audio is not recorded or uploaded. No account required. The app is free to download and shows banner ads until Premium is active.

1. Who we are

Decibel Meter Pro is published by Lucky Tools (Northern Ireland).

2. Microphone

The app requests microphone access to compute sound level (dB) in real time. Audio samples are processed on device for metering only. We do not save raw audio files or upload audio to Lucky Tools servers.

3. On-device data

Calibration offset and preferences stay in private app storage. Clear storage or uninstall to remove them.

4. Advertising

This app is free to download and includes the Google AdMob advertising SDK, which serves banner ads. While a Premium subscription is active, ads are not shown. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers to select ads, as described in Google's privacy policy.

5. Subscriptions

Decibel Meter Pro offers an optional recurring subscription to remove advertisements and access premium features. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play and can be managed or canceled anytime in your Google Play account settings.

Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited session length, more sessions per day, longer history retention and data export. Payment records stay with Google Play; we never see your payment details.

6. Children

Not directed at children under 13.

7. Deletion

See data deletion. Email autoaccentsni@gmail.com with questions.

8. Disclaimer

Phone microphones are not laboratory SPL meters. Not for safety certification.

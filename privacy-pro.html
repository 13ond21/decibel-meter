1. Who we are

Decibel Meter Pro is published by Lucky Tools (Northern Ireland).

2. Microphone

The app requests microphone access to compute sound level (dB) in real time. Audio samples are processed on device for metering only. We do not save raw audio files or upload audio to Lucky Tools servers.

3. On-device data

Calibration offset and preferences stay in private app storage. Clear storage or uninstall to remove them.

4. No ads

The Pro build does not include AdMob for serving ads.

5. Purchases

Pro is a one-time paid app on Google Play. Payments are handled by Google. No in-app subscriptions.
